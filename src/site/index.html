  1. Weigh in

    Gross and tare at the scale. Each dealer's price tier is applied automatically, and the seller's ID is checked on the spot.

  2. Pay

    Held tickets wait at the cashier. Checks are numbered per yard, ACH is supported, and the Positive Pay file goes straight to your bank.

  3. Count

    Inventory by yard and commodity updates the moment a ticket closes. No end-of-day re-keying.

  4. Ship

    Contracts, purchase orders, outbound loads, trailers and freight costs, with gross profit on every shipment.

  5. File

    BOLs, signed disclosures and price agreements attach to the ticket or dealer they belong to.

Built for the scale house and the office

Built for the way a multi-yard scrap company actually works.

Seller ID that's actually protected

Driver's license number and date of birth are encrypted. License photo and thumbprint are stored with the dealer, USB fingerprint readers are supported, and every time someone looks at an ID it's logged.

Dealer pricing tiers

Scale, D2, D1 and D-High price levels from your daily price sheet, with yard-by-yard adjustments. The scale operator never picks a price by hand.

Checks your bank can verify

Sequential check numbers per account, a check register with cleared and outstanding balances, and a Positive Pay file in the format your bank already takes.

Freight and trailers

Carriers, lanes and trailers in one place, with the freight cost on every outbound load so gross profit is real, not estimated.

Seller ID and thumbprints

Capture the license photo with a tablet camera and the thumbprint with a USB fingerprint reader, right at the window.

Works at the scale and in the yard

Runs in a browser on the scale-house PC, a tablet at the window, or a phone out by the piles. Nothing to install.

Paperwork with expiry dates

Insurance, permits and price agreements are stored with the record they belong to, and the Documents tab warns you 60 days before they lapse.

Reports that catch problems

Purchases by commodity and dealer, payables and receivables aging, contract status, and an exceptions report for duplicate dealers and suspicious tickets.

Built for companies with more than one yard

Running several yards usually means separate systems, separate logins and a spreadsheet to tie them together. Yard-Built was made by people who run several yards at once, so each location keeps its own scale, cashier and bank account while the owner sees the whole company in one place.

  • Switch yards in one click or view the whole company
  • One price sheet with adjustments by yard
  • A bank account per yard one Positive Pay file for all
  • Staff see their own yards owners see everything
  • Company-wide reports or one yard at a time
